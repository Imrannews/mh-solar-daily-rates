# Suggested starter rates

The app intentionally reads rates from Firestore rather than hard-coding them. Add the products you actually sell in Firebase Console or through the Admin panel.

Recommended categories:
- Solar Panel
- Inverter
- Battery
- Solar Accessories

Example panel records can include Jinko 585W/590W, Canadian Solar, LONGi, JA Solar and AIKO. Enter the current price yourself because market rates change daily.
